About Sweet Crumbs

Sweet Crumbs Bakery is a fictional neighborhood bakery created for a web development project. Our goal is to make ordering baked goods feel welcoming and simple.

We believe a bakery website should help visitors quickly learn about the business, browse the menu, and get in touch.

Our promise: Friendly service, delicious ideas, and a simple experience on any device.
